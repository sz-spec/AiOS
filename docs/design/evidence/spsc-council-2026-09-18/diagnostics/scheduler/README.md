## Records and window

User arrays are bounded to 512 records per role, with dropped-event counts. Kind 1 is entry into full (producer) or empty (consumer); kind 2 is first subsequent successful push/pop. Full-yield attempts and empty-period starts are counted separately. TSC and independently acquired head/tail samples accompany each transition; this is not an atomic head/tail snapshot. Consumer recording is gated off before arming and after `t_end`. Producer full counts cover the timed loop, not sentinel retries. A consumer empty period already ongoing at arm is observed on its next poll; the record does not establish its true beginning. Dumps occur only after observed consumer completion; timeout preserves failure without racing its still-active array.

A diagnostic-only GETPID convention uses RDI `0x5350534344494147`, RDX `0x453745364f4e4c59`, RSI 1=arm/2=stop. Ordinary GETPID is unchanged. No user output buffer/new canonical ABI is introduced. Recorder refuses non-BSP/multiple-CPU topology. It is deliberately **UP only**, not an SMP-safe diagnostic API or security product interface. Arm records caller TID; only that TID may stop. It must not be enabled in a release kernel.

Kernel envelope starts just before arm returns and ends inside stop, enclosing canonical `t_start..t_end` plus syscall boundary overhead. It counts IRQ0 handler entries during that envelope, not inferred tick counts. Reason-tagged reschedule calls are 1=timer IRQ, 2=yield, 0=other. Counts of switches require `next != prev`, rather than existing slice-expiry statistics. Events record the selected previous/next TIDs immediately before `do_context_switch`; they are a kernel selection witness, not an independent hardware proof of resumed user execution. The original `do_context_switch` sanity checks and machinery remain active. `prior_slice` is selected task's value **before reset**. `granted_slice` is read directly from `next->time_slice` after the existing reset and AI multipliers inside `do_context_switch`; it is not inferred from a copied formula.

Arm captures current plus queued task identities, state, priority and slices under scheduler lock with IRQs disabled. Limits: 64 stored identities and at most 64 links traversed per queue; overflow is explicit. The arm census is kept separately. At every armed reschedule selection, including same-task selections and after event-buffer exhaustion, a bounded scan samples the selected next task plus all queued tasks, deduplicates TIDs, accumulates min/max runnable counts, and records up to 64 distinct seen identities. `scan_drop` reports traversal/storage truncation and `seen_drop` counts observations of identities that could not be stored (not a unique omitted-identity count). Min/max are sampled queue counts, not continuous extrema; nonzero scan_drop makes them lower-bound observations. This is not a full task-registry census, and TID identity assumes no reuse within the short window. Idle may be included when selected, explicitly identified by its recorded identity/state rather than silently removed. Kernel events cap at 2048 with drop count. Any overflow prevents a complete event-trajectory claim.
