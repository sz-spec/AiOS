- **P2 — correctness-first TLB cost is explicit.** The revised shootdown invalidates all relevant contexts rather than stepping through a range. This covers small-page mappings safely but may discard substantially more useful translations. No benchmark here quantifies that tradeoff or justifies a fastest-system claim.
- **P2 — deferred reclamation trades latency for safe context.** AS final release enqueues retirement; the process-context drain releases page tables and backing references. This keeps filesystem teardown out of root-switch critical sections, but queue growth and reclamation latency require measurement under sustained clone/exec pressure.
- **P2 — fixed ownership limits balancing.** Gated task ownership prevents migration-related stack/FPU hazards. It can leave one CPU busy while another lacks eligible work; observed AP execution does not establish load-balancing efficiency.
